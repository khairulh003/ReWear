Our mission

Clothing shouldn't go to waste
while people go without.

ReWear exists because two problems sit side by side: usable clothes are thrown away every day, and many people nearby still can't access enough of what they need. We built a simple way to close that gap - one event at a time.

We don't run a warehouse or a logistics company. We run a community calendar: donation drives where members drop off clothes, and thrift swaps where members trade what they no longer wear. Every event is hosted at our facility, organised by volunteers, and open to anyone who wants to help.

♻️Reduce textile waste

Fast fashion is one of the largest consumers of fresh water globally. Every swapped or donated item is one less thing in a landfill.

🤝Meet real need

When organisations tell us what's needed, like warm clothing for displaced families, we turn that into an event our members can act on.

📍One facility, real events

Everything happens at our community space. No shipping, no middlemen. Just people showing up for each other.

🌱Built by members

Anyone can join as a member. From there, you choose how involved you want to be; drop off clothes, or roll up your sleeves and volunteer.
